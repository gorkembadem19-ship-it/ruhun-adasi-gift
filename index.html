Ruhun Adası Gift is a digital education and humanitarian support project. We share practical e-commerce learning resources while building a transparent initiative intended to fund food and essential supplies for communities in Africa.

Practical digital education

Our educational content focuses on practical e-commerce fundamentals such as online store setup, product research, supplier relationships, customer experience and digital business basics. We do not promise guaranteed earnings or instant success.

Purpose-led project

Humanitarian support in Africa

Our current plan is to direct qualifying contributions toward the purchase of food and essential supplies, working with suppliers and logistics partners to deliver assistance to communities in need. As the initiative develops, we intend to publish updates and evidence of completed support activities.

We are not presenting Ruhun Adası Gift as a registered charity unless and until the appropriate legal status and documentation are in place.

1. Contributions
Payments are intended to support the stated humanitarian purpose.
2. Procurement
Food and essential supplies are planned to be purchased from established suppliers.
3. Delivery & transparency
We plan to document and share completed assistance activities where appropriate.
