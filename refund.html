Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Live payment functionality is not currently enabled on this website. This policy will be updated before payments are activated.

Educational purchases

If paid educational products or services are introduced, the applicable refund conditions will be clearly disclosed before purchase and will follow applicable consumer-protection requirements.

Humanitarian contributions

If voluntary humanitarian contributions are enabled, any refund availability will depend on the payment status, applicable law, and the payment provider's rules. Contributions that have already been used to procure or deliver assistance may not be recoverable.

Payment provider

Where a third-party provider processes payments, refunds may also be subject to that provider's procedures, eligibility rules, and processing times.
