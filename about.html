About Ruhun Adası Gift

Ruhun Adası Gift is a developing digital education and humanitarian support project. Our educational content focuses on practical e-commerce and digital business fundamentals.

Alongside this educational work, we are developing a humanitarian initiative intended to support communities in Africa through food and essential-supply procurement. We plan to work with suppliers and logistics partners and to publish updates as completed activities become available.

Transparency note

Ruhun Adası Gift does not claim registered-charity status unless supported by the appropriate legal registration and documentation. Payment and humanitarian statements on this website are intended to reflect the current project plan and will be updated as the project develops.
